COACHING FAQ

Common Questions

Explore helpful information about Splashes coaching programs.

What should swimmers bring?

Swimmers should bring a swimsuit, goggles, towel and water bottle.

How are swimmers assigned to a level?

Coaches evaluate swimming ability, technique and comfort in the water before recommending the appropriate program level.

Can beginners participate?

Yes. Splashes offers programs designed specifically for beginning swimmers who are developing confidence and foundational swimming skills.

Are competitive programs available?

Yes. Advanced swimmers can participate in training focused on race preparation, starts, turns, endurance and competitive strategy.
