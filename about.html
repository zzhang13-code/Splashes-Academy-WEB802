About Splashes

A swimmer centered club built around skill, confidence, competition and community.

WHO WE ARE

Every swimmer deserves a lane to grow.

Splashes is a fictional swimming company created for this WEB801 project. The concept combines professional coaching, structured tournaments and community activities within one responsive digital experience.

Our imagined coaching philosophy focuses on safe progression, measurable technique, positive competition and long term enjoyment of the sport.
